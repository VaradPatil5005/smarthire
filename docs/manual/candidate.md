# User manual: candidates and consultants

## Join and log in

Your organisation gives you an invitation code. Go to `/join/`, enter the code, choose a username and
a password. A code works once and expires. After that, log in at `/login/`.

## Complete your profile

Open **My profile**. Name and title are required. Your notice period is optional; if you leave it
blank, your Staffing fit shows "availability unknown" rather than a low number.

## Add skills by form

Pick a skill from the list and, if you can, the months you used it. These show as **Self-reported**.
You never need a résumé to complete your profile, so this works for a scanned or non-English résumé
too.

## Upload a résumé

Read the notice on the page first. Upload a PDF of up to 5 MB and 10 pages.

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Being checked | The file is in quarantine while it is scanned | Wait, then refresh |
| Processed | The file was read | Nothing. A reviewer looks at it next |
| Could not be processed | The file could not be read, and the message says why | Follow the message: remove a password, export again, or use a smaller file. You can always use the skill form |

A new upload creates a new version. Earlier versions stay. If a new file fails, your profile, skills
and last good version do not change.

Files that are not PDFs, or are over 5 MB, are refused straight away with a message.

## Correct or delete your data

Under **Your data**, choose **Ask for a correction** or **Ask for deletion**. The request goes to a
staff member. You can also edit your profile, add skills or upload a new version at any time.

# ADR 0004: Coverage and Staffing fit stay separate

Status: accepted, 2026-11-08.

## Decision

Merit (Coverage) and timing (Staffing fit) are two numbers. Neither alters the other. The optional combined view stays out until the mentor agrees a split (D-002).

## Why

An excellent architect on a 60-day notice period is not a weaker engineer, only a later one.

## Cost

A delivery manager reads two columns instead of one.
